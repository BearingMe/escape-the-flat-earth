# Checkpoint — M14 Crafting

**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** complete

M14 implements data-driven crafting recipes, expedition tool fabrication, shared multiplayer crafting stations, and durability tracking under host-authoritative atomic inventory transactions.

## Delivered Capabilities
- **Crafting Recipe Architecture (`CraftingRecipe`)**:
  - Validated data-driven recipes specifying recipe IDs, display names, station requirements (hand-crafted vs. workbench), multi-ingredient dictionaries, output item IDs, output counts, and default durability.
  - Atomic ingredient verification and consumption against `InventoryContainer`.
- **Expedition Tech Tree & Catalog (`CraftingCatalog`)**:
  - Implements useful and ridiculous flat-earth survival contraptions:
    - `craft_medical_kit`: basic hand-crafted salvage medicine from scrap and lumber.
    - `craft_climbing_grapple`: reinforced grapple spikes for scaling steep obstacles and the Ice Wall.
    - `craft_thermal_insulation`: perimeter thermal wraps providing cold resistance at the edge.
    - `craft_steam_megaphone`: high-pressure sonic push device crafted from scrap and electronics.
    - `craft_firmament_sensor`: exotic geodetic detector crafted from tech salvage, ice crystals, and void obsidian.
  - Recipe discovery and query methods (`get_available_recipes`).
- **World Expedition Crafting Station (`CraftingStation`)**:
  - Shared physical workbench prop (`crafting_station.tscn`).
  - Host-authoritative execution: validates recipe ingredients, executes atomic transfers, and signals completion.
  - Concurrency safety: simultaneous player craft attempts on limited materials cannot duplicate outputs or double-spend inputs.
