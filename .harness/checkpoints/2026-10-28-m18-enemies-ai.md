# Checkpoint — M18 Enemies & AI

**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** complete

M18 delivers five thematically absurd, mechanically distinct enemy archetypes adhering strictly to flat-earth lore played 100% straight, driven by host-authoritative perception, pursuit steering, unique combat mechanics, and leak-free defeat salvage drops.

## Delivered Capabilities
- **Five Distinct Enemy Archetypes (`EnemyArchetypeDefinition`)**:
  1. *Firmament Geodetic Drone* (`geodetic_drone.tres`): High-speed aerial patrol unit in Plains that charges and delivers ramming damage with physics knockback.
  2. *Perimeter Ice Sentinel* (`ice_sentinel.tres`): Heavy high-HP brute stationed along the Tundra and Ice Wall that hurls physical ice boulder projectiles at range.
  3. *Void Abyss Swarmer* (`void_swarmer.tres`): Rapid, erratic pack predator on the Underside and deep forests that flanks and saps player stamina upon biting.
  4. *Sub-Disk Patrol Rover* (`patrol_rover.tres`): Wheeled autonomous interceptor patrolling the rim and underside that discharges pressurized steam bursts pushing players backward.
  5. *Magnetic North Singularity* (`magnetic_anomaly.tres`): Dense metallic anomaly in the Mountain peaks that exerts dynamic inverse-distance magnetic pull on players and metal salvage.
- **AI Perception & Steering Framework (`AISteeringModel`)**:
  - Horizontal pursuit and flee velocity integration.
  - Squared-distance perception queries and line-of-sight checks.
  - Continuous magnetic pull force calculation with distance falloff.
- **Host-Authoritative Enemy Entity (`EnemyEntity`)**:
  - `CharacterBody3D` driven by a 4-state utility machine (`IDLE`, `CHASE`, `ATTACK`, `DEFEATED`).
  - Target player acquisition from `"network_players"` with automatic aggro transfer when damaged by rival players.
  - Physics prop damage reception from M10 impact model; clean despawning and salvage drop instantiation upon defeat without entity memory leaks.
